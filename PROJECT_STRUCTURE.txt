What the project is
  A Python simulation in which Scout Drones, Firefighters and a Coordinator work
  together to contain a wind-driven wildfire on a 50 x 50 forest grid. Scouts map
  the fire into a shared belief map, the Coordinator groups the fire into zones and
  auctions them, and firefighters bid, travel with risk-aware A* and fight the fire.

    |

    |-- model.py                   WildfireModel: builds the world and agents, runs the six
    |                              stages of every step in a fixed order, computes metrics
    |                              and the Review 1 score.
    |-- belief_map.py              The shared belief map: last seen state and time of every
    |                              cell; newest observation wins; stale after 15 steps.
    |-- messages.py                The message types (OBSERVE, TARGET, ANNOUNCE, BID, AWARD,
    |                              DONE, REVOKE) and the message bus that delivers them.
    |-- strategies.py              The three strategies compared: independent, greedy, auction.
    |-- scenarios.py               The six Review 1 test scenarios as config overrides.
    |-- settings.py                Loads config.yaml and merges scenario overrides onto it.
    |
    |-- environment/               THE WORLD
    |   |-- cells.py               Cell states (Tree, Burning, Burnt, Firebreak, Water, Empty)
    |   |                          and grid distance helpers.
    |   |-- forest.py              Builds the forest map: lakes, dense/sparse trees, the river
    |   |                          preset, ignition points, firefighter start cells.
    |   `-- fire.py                The fire-spread formula p_base x fuel x (1 + k cos theta),
    |                              the spread step, and the risk map used by A*.
    |
    |-- agents/                    THE THREE AGENT TYPES
    |   |-- scout.py               Scout Drone: senses radius 3, picks frontier targets,
    |   |                          reports what it sees.
    |   |-- firefighter.py         Firefighter: bids for zones, navigates with A*, extinguishes,
    |   |                          cuts firebreaks, refills water, safety rules, fallback when
    |   |                          the Coordinator is silent.
    |   `-- coordinator.py         Coordinator: clusters fire into zones, runs the auctions,
    |                              re-assigns firefighters that are absent or freed.
    |
    |-- algorithms/                THE ALGORITHMS (plain functions, tested directly)
    |   |-- astar.py               Risk-aware A* search and uniform-cost search (for comparison).
    |   |-- auction.py             Bid utility, sequential single-item auction, greedy baseline.
    |   |-- frontier.py            Frontier-based exploration target selection for scouts.
    |   `-- zones.py               Fire-zone clustering, stable zone ids, threat score,
    |                              firebreak targets.
    |
    |-- app.py                     The live demo (Solara in the browser): map, belief map,
    |                              auction log, metrics, and the "Compare all 3 strategies"
    |                              button.
    |-- run_experiments.py         Main experiment: 6 scenarios x 3 strategies x 30 seeds

    |-- run_studies.py             Supporting studies behind the design decisions (fire time

    |                              -> results/studies/.

    |   |-- summary.csv            Mean and spread of every metric per scenario and strategy.

    |   |-- forest_saved_by_scenario.png, paired_gains.png, scaling.png, astar_vs_ucs.png

HOW THE PIECES CONNECT
======================
  config.yaml --> settings.py --> model.py --+--> environment/   (the forest and the fire)
                                             +--> agents/        (scouts, firefighters, Coordinator)
                                             |        |
                                             |        +--> algorithms/   (A*, auction, frontier, zones)
                                             |        +--> belief_map.py + messages.py (shared map, messages)
                                             |
            app.py (live demo) --------------+
            run_experiments.py, run_studies.py --> results/ --> slides and study guide


QUICK COMMANDS (from wildfire_squad/)

  setup.bat                          install everything (Windows; ./setup.sh elsewhere)
  run_demo.bat                       live demo at http://localhost:8765
  run_tests.bat                      all 114 tests
  run_experiments.bat --quick        30-second experiment check
